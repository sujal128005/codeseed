The idea behind Tathvi

Tathvi comes from तथ्य, fact. Here’s how every video is made, and what you’ll find.

The Tathvi way

Four steps. Every story.

Most news tells you what happened. We find out why, who gains, and what the numbers really say.

01

Question the headline

Who is saying it, why now, and who benefits if you believe it?

02

Follow the money

Revenue, losses, debt and incentives. Numbers turned into clear charts and maps.

03

Check the source

Company filings, government data, books and reports, shown on screen so you can check them yourself.

04

Give the verdict

Every video ends with a clear answer. And if we ever get a fact wrong, it’s fixed on the Corrections page with the timestamp.
